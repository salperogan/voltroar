What is VoltRoar?

VoltRoar connects over Bluetooth LE to an OBD adapter plugged into your car, reads your speed and accelerator pedal, and synthesizes an engine sound that revs, shifts gears and roars along with you — choose from a V4, V6, V8, V10, V12, a big-turbo inline-6 or a high-revving flat-6, with turbo whistle, blow-off and backfire crackle. Sound plays through your iPhone or your car's speakers, also in the background.

Tip: use a cable for zero delay. Connect your iPhone to the car with a cable — wired CarPlay or wired audio if possible. Wireless connections (Bluetooth, wireless CarPlay) can add a serious delay between the pedal and the sound. There is also a “Lower music while playing” option so the engine stands out over your music.

VoltRoar is read-only: it never writes to your car or changes any setting — it only reads the data your car provides over its standard OBD-II port.

No car? Manual mode lets you tap Start Engine and drag an on-screen throttle pedal anywhere.

FAQ

My adapter doesn't connect. What should I do?

A genuine (original) ELM327 or Fnirsi Bluetooth LE adapter is required. Clone adapters were tested and do not work. Also check that the adapter is fully plugged into the OBD port, the car is switched on, Bluetooth is enabled for VoltRoar in iOS Settings, and the adapter is not connected to another app. Do not pair it in iOS Settings; connect from inside VoltRoar.

Connection takes a while on first connect.

On BYD vehicles VoltRoar first wakes up the car's diagnostic gateway. Progress is shown on screen; this can take some seconds.

Does it work with my non-BYD car?

You can try it: choose Other in the vehicle list. VoltRoar then connects over standard OBD-II and reads speed and accelerator for the engine sound. This is experimental (beta) and may not work on every car — it depends on the vehicle's ECU model, production year and software. Named brands shown as “coming soon” will get full support in future updates. Manual mode works on any iPhone, with no car.

What's the difference between Pro and Max?

Pro unlocks all engine sounds, all effects and the Gauges page. Max adds the Battery and Performance pages — and includes everything in Pro. Both are one-time, lifetime purchases.

Can VoltRoar harm my car?

No. VoltRoar is read-only. It only reads the data the car provides over its standard OBD-II port; it never writes to the vehicle, changes settings or clears codes.

Does the sound keep playing when the screen is locked?

Yes. VoltRoar keeps playing in the background. When music is playing you can turn on “Lower music while playing” so the engine stands out.

I bought Pro or Max on another device, or reinstalled the app.

Open the purchase screen in the app and tap Restore Purchases, signed in with the same Apple ID.

Is VoltRoar a pedestrian warning sound?

No. VoltRoar is for entertainment and does not replace your car's legally required pedestrian warning sound (AVAS).
