VoltRoar Privacy Policy

Effective date: 30 October 2026

Summary

VoltRoar does not collect, store on any server, share or sell any personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. Everything the app does happens on your iPhone.

Data we collect

None. VoltRoar does not collect personal information such as your name, email address, location, contacts, identifiers or usage data. The app contains no analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs and does not require an account.

Bluetooth and vehicle data

VoltRoar uses Bluetooth only to communicate with the OBD adapter plugged into your car. It reads vehicle data such as speed, accelerator pedal position and 12V battery voltage in order to generate the engine sound and show gauges. This data is processed in real time on your device and is never transmitted to us or to any third party. VoltRoar only reads data and never writes to your vehicle.

Motion sensor

The G-force meter on the Performance page uses your iPhone's motion sensor. Motion data is used only on your device while the page is in use and is not stored or transmitted.

Purchases

The optional VoltRoar Pro purchase is processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive your payment information. The app checks your purchase status with Apple on the device. Apple's handling of your data is described in Apple's privacy policy.

Settings stored on your device

Your preferences (for example the selected engine, effects and volume) are stored locally on your iPhone. They are removed when you delete the app.

Children

VoltRoar does not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new effective date.
